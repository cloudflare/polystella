Admin layout: Collections and per-language Translation settings are closed-by-default cards in two columns, opened from their top-right control (turning a collection on opens it). The Sandbox splits into settings, source and output cards. The Catalog page lists groups alphabetically on the left and shows the chosen group on the right. Batch translation puts its options beside the entry list, and for a whole collection shows its entry counts and how many entries each locale is missing.
